# Code of Conduct

Be direct, be kind, assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people. Keep
reviews about the change. Anyone — maintainer or contributor — who is harassed
or made unwelcome here can write to conduct@epoint.ro and it will be handled
privately and promptly.

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
